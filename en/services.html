Services

From permit to furniture

The whole project, from one partner.

With many years in the construction and renovation of homes, hotels and commercial spaces, we deliver spaces with contemporary quality, ecological sense, energy savings and durability.

  • Design & permits

    We start on paper. We handle planning permits, architectural design and interiors, and show you the space in photorealistic 3D before the first crew arrives.

    • Planning permits
    • Architectural design and studies
    • Interior design
    • Photorealistic 3D

  • Construction & renovation

    Homes, hotels and commercial spaces, from structure to final coat. One crew, one person accountable, and a schedule that is met before the season opens.

    • Dry construction
    • Floors, marble, stone
    • Doors and windows
    • Painting and finishes
    • Insulation

  • Systems & energy

    What you don't see keeps a building alive. We design and install the mechanical and electrical systems and upgrade the envelope, so the space lasts longer and costs less to run.

    • Heating, cooling, ventilation
    • Electrical
    • Plumbing and drainage
    • Fire safety, lifts
    • External insulation, boilers

  • Wood atelier

    We don't pick furniture from a catalogue. We design it and make it by hand in natural wood, to the measure of the room and the person.

    • Handmade furniture
    • Kitchens
    • Wardrobes
    • Doors

  • Outdoor spaces

    Gardens, courtyards and pools that carry the house out towards the view.

    • Gardens
    • Pools
    • Courtyards

  • Digital presence

    For hotels and businesses, we deliver the website that shows the space along with the space itself.

    • Business website

How we work

  1. We listen

    To the site, the budget, and the way you want to live or host.

  2. We design

    Architectural design and 3D, until you see exactly what you will receive.

  3. We license

    We take on planning permits and every approval.

  4. We build

    With our own crews and one project lead from start to finish.

  5. We furnish

    Our atelier makes the furniture for that specific space.

  6. We hand over

    A finished space, on time, ready to live in.
