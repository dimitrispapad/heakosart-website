Studio

Architect, engineer, designer, builder

We think like architects. We build like craftsmen.

TheakosArt is a team where the drawing board and the building site speak the same language.

The architect, the electrical engineer and the designer sit at the same table as the person who signs the handover. Each decision is made once and made well, and you have one person to talk to about everything.

We work mostly in Mykonos, where time is short and standards are high. That is where we learned to promise only what we deliver.
